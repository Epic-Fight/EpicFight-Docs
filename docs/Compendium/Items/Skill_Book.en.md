crafting:
tips: |
  * Skill Book textures reflect the skill category of the skill that they hold.
  * Different mobs may drop different skills based on difficulty.
  * Bosses such as the Wither & Ender Dragon may hold special skills that are only obtainable after slaying them.
trivia: |
  * Skill Books were intially only meant to be found inside of dungeons, but that was later scrapped as they'd clutter chests.  
  * Each Skill Book texture reflects a certain skill category, the dagger = passives, the shield = guard, the target = mobility, the person = identity and the blowing wind = dodge.
