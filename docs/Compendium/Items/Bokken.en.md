trivia: |
  * "Bokken" means wooden sword in Japanese.
  * The Bokken was originally added as a wooden variant of the Katana(now [Uchigatana]), and was meant to be implemented in a unimplemented training system, where the user would learn how to fight with a Sensei/Skill Master.
  * The Bokken's original name was "Wooden Training Sword".
  * The Bokken later became a tutorial weapon for mod/pack developers who could be interested in creating their own weapon types without the need of code.
  * It'd make it's first appearance back in 20.6.1, when the experimental datapack feature, now knows as the Datapack editor, begun it's development. This experiment, later led to the addition of [NBT Weapon Assigning].
